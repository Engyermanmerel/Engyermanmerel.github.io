Engineering Stack

Product Development Lifecycle

Stage 01

Conceptual & System Design

Systems Engineering Foundations

  • Requirements Traceability (PRD to SYRS)
  • System Architecture Design (Functional & Physical)
  • Functional Decomposition & Flow Block Diagrams
  • Interference & Interface Management
  • Safety and Reliability Goal Setting

Concept Selection & Analysis

  • Brainstorming & Pugh Matrix Evaluation
  • First-Order Calculations & Hand Specs
  • Material Selection (Fundamental Properties)
  • Initial Design FMEA (DFMEA)
  • Trade-off Analysis for System Parameters
Stage 02

Detailed Design & Prototyping

Advanced 3D CAD Modeling

  • Parametric & Assembly Modeling (Top-Down Design)
  • Design Rational Documentation
  • Subsystem Design Integration
  • Advanced Tolerance / Stack-up Analysis
  • GD&T Application (ASME Y14.5 / ISO)

Simulation & Optimization

  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA - Static & Thermal)
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD - Initial)
  • Multi-Body Dynamics (MBD)
  • Subsystem Verification Testing Plans
  • Numerical optimization of system parameters
Stage 03

Manufacturing & Realization

Design for Manufacturing & Sustenance

  • DFMA / PFMEA Execution
  • Material Specification & Procurement Sheet (BOM)
  • Critical-to-Quality (CTQ) Parameter Identification
  • Change Control & File Storage Management
  • Risk-Based Product Support Planning

Verification & Product Launch

  • Verification/Validation Testing (DVP&R)
  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA - 8D/5 Whys)
  • Transfer to Manufacturing Documentation
  • Rational for inspection dimensions
  • Human Factor & Ergonomics Studies
